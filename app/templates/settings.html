Geographic scope

Research from every region and context matters, and a healthy literature includes all of those voices. Since this project has so far been developed by a U.S.-based researcher as a tool with (mostly) other American scholars in mind thus far, testing has demonstrated that including a global reach can produce less relevant results for some scholars — that's the only reason this option exists. However, I am sensitive to the way this risks further perpetuating inequalities that already favor scholarship from W.E.I.R.D. countries and welcome any input and testing on ways to further incorporate non-Western voices into this system in ways that remain relevant to those scholars whose research focuses on Western contexts. When it's on, papers from venues based outside North America, Western, Northern and Southern Europe, Australia and New Zealand are filtered out, and papers focused on non-Western contexts are weighed somewhat lower — though strong, highly relevant work can still make your briefing. Toggling this re-judges the current window of papers on the next run.
