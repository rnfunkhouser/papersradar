How it works

Two stages, both inspectable: Gathering casts the net, Selection reads what was caught.

Describe your work

Write down your interests in your own words and add a few papers you wish you'd been alerted to. Setup takes about five minutes.

Daily gathering & shortlist

Each morning the pipeline searches the research areas your seed papers point to and shortlists new work that sits closest to them by semantic similarity, not keywords.

An AI judge reads the shortlist

The judge scores each shortlisted abstract against your Selection Criteria. Papers that clear your bar appear in your dashboard and, if you want, your inbox — rationale attached.
